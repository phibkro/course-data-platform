TDT4136 Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Studiepoeng

7.5

Undervisningsspråk

Engelsk

Faglig innhold

Search and knowledge representation, planning, and reasoning under uncertainty. Introduction to intelligent agents, problem solving by search, and machine learning fundamentals.

Læringsutbytte

The student can apply foundational AI methods to model and solve problems, including search algorithms, knowledge representation, and probabilistic reasoning.

Læringsformer og aktiviteter

Forelesninger og øvinger. Some exercises are completed individually and some are completed in groups of up to three students.

Mer om vurdering

Skoleeksamen teller 100 prosent av karakteren. Ikke obligatorisk oppmøte i forelesning.

Øvinger må være godkjent for å kunne gå opp til eksamen.
